Common questions

  • Why can't I pick where a song starts? BC Radio is a live station. Everyone hears the same song at the same moment, so the app joins wherever the station is right now.
  • No sound? Check the volume, and that your phone isn't connected to a speaker or headphones you didn't expect. The app plays even with the silent switch on.
  • Can I listen with the screen locked? Yes. Start playing and lock your phone. The controls show up on the lock screen and in Control Center.
  • How do requests work? Tap Request, pick a song, and the station adds it to the queue and tells you about when it'll play. You can make one request every couple of minutes. Songs that just played need a break before they can come back.
  • The request button says to try again. A quick check keeps bots off the request line. It usually passes without you seeing it. If it fails, wait a moment and try again on a steady connection.
